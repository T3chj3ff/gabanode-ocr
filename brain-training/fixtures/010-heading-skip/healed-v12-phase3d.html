Permit Application Guide

This guide explains how to apply for a building permit in Maplewood.

Step 1: Gather Documents

You will need to submit the following documents with your application.

  • Completed application form
  • Proof of property ownership
  • Site plan or plot map

Step 2: Submit Application

Applications may be submitted online, by mail, or in person at City Hall.

Online Submission

Visit the city portal at cityofmaplewood.gov/permits to submit your application electronically.

In-Person Submission

City Hall is open Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm.

Step 3: Pay Fees

Permit fees are based on the estimated value of the construction project.

Fee Schedule

Base fee: $75 plus $5 per $1,000 of project value over $10,000.

Step 4: Await Approval

Standard processing time is 10-15 business days.
