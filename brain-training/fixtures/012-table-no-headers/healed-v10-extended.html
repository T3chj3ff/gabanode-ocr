Fee Schedule

The following fees apply to permit applications submitted to the City of Maplewood.

Building Permits

Residential — New Construction $150 base + $5 per $1,000 value 10-15 business days
Residential — Addition $100 base + $4 per $1,000 value 7-10 business days
Commercial — New Construction $300 base + $8 per $1,000 value 15-20 business days
Commercial — Renovation $200 base + $6 per $1,000 value 10-15 business days

Zoning Variances

Minor Variance $250 30 days
Major Variance $500 60 days
Special Use Permit $750 90 days

Inspection Fees

Initial Inspection Included with permit Scheduled after approval
Re-inspection $75 per occurrence 5 business days notice
