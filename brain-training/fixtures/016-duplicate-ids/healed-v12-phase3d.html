Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all City of Maplewood employees.

Section 1: Employment Policies

All employees are expected to adhere to the city's code of conduct.

Refer to Section 2 for compensation details.

Section 2: Compensation

Salaries are reviewed annually based on performance and budget availability.

  • Base salary determined by grade level
  • Merit increases up to 5% annually
  • Cost-of-living adjustments applied January 1

Section 3: Benefits

The city provides a comprehensive benefits package including health, dental, and vision.

Enrollment occurs during the annual open enrollment period each November.
